Accounting Transparency & Categorization:
1. Recorded Activity Data:

Logged by the user with documented emission factors (e.g. CEA grid factor 0.716 kg/kWh). These constitute the primary footprint baseline.

2. Verified Evidence Reductions:

Actions backed by uploaded tickets/meter bills inspected and approved by the sustainability reviewer.

3. Simulated Reductions:

What-if scenarios generated in the Simulator for planning purposes. They are strictly kept separate and not counted in the official ledger.
