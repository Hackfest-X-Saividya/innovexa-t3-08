Official Data Sources & Factors

Green Pulse uses verified, scientifically documented emission factors without invented metrics or unvalidated claims.

Electricity Grid Baseline: 0.716 kg CO2/kWh
Source: Central Electricity Authority (CEA), Ministry of Power, Govt. of India. CO2 Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector, Version 20.0 (2024). Weighted average of all grid-connected thermal, hydro, nuclear, and RE plants.
Transportation Factors: 0.054 to 0.170 kg CO2e/km
Source: UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ) / DEFRA 2023 greenhouse gas reporting conversion factors, adjusted with ARAI Indian road condition commuter metrics.
LPG & Fuels: 2.983 kg CO2e/kg (LPG) & 2.31 kg/L (Petrol)
Source: IPCC 2006 Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories, Energy Volume, Stationary and Mobile Combustion. 1 standard domestic 14.2 kg LPG cylinder = ~42.36 kg CO2e.
Waste Methane Commitment: 0.580 kg CO2e/kg
Source: IPCC Waste Model for solid waste disposal. Unsegregated organic waste decomposing anaerobically in unmanaged landfills generates fugitive CH4 (28x GWP of CO2). Composting diverts this to aerobic low-carbon decay (0.100 kg CO2e/kg).

Calculation Assumptions & Limitations

  • Calculations represent defensible estimates, not direct real-time laboratory gas spectrometry measurements.
  • Bus emission factor assumes an average city occupancy of 40 passengers. If a bus runs empty, per-passenger emissions vary.
  • Electricity bill estimations are based on default state slab tariff (₹7.00/kWh) and do not include fixed meter demand charges or local municipal duties.
  • To avoid double-counting, electricity consumed for charging electric vehicles should be logged either under Transport EV or Electricity, not both.
